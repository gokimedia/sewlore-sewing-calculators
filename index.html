Sewing measurement calculators

Arithmetic for your own readings. Use the same unit and the same marked points within each fabric test. A decimal point or decimal comma is accepted; do not use thousands separators.

Fabric stretch and recovery

Record one marked direction before stretching, while stretched, and after release. These readings do not determine suitable garment ease or replace a fabric test.

Formulas and hypothetical example

Stretch = 100 × (stretched − original) ÷ original. Residual change = 100 × (released − original) ÷ original. Recovered extension = 100 × (stretched − released) ÷ (stretched − original).

For original 10 cm, stretched 14 cm and released 10.5 cm: stretch is 40%, residual change is 5%, and recovered extension is 87.5%. No sample was measured for this example.

Button-centre intervals

Offsets locate the first centre from the top and the last centre from the bottom. Use 2–100 centres. Check closure instructions, seam allowances and buttonhole sizes before marking.

Formula and hypothetical example

Interval = (opening length − top offset − bottom offset) ÷ (number of centres − 1). Position i = top offset + i × interval, with i starting at zero.

A 60 cm opening, 3 cm offsets at both ends and 7 centres give six 9 cm intervals. Positions from the top are 3, 12, 21, 30, 39, 48 and 57 cm. No garment was tested for this example.

Length unit conversion

Enter a non-negative length. This converter changes the unit; the unit menus in the other tools only label the readings you enter.

Conversion definitions

1 cm = 10 mm. 1 international inch = exactly 25.4 mm. For example, 2.54 cm converts to 1 in. Conversion does not improve measurement precision.

Install the WordPress plugin

Upload the version 0.1.0 plugin ZIP through Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, activate it, and insert this into the built-in Shortcode block:

[sewlore_sewing_calculators]

Choose tool="stretch", tool="buttons" or tool="units" to display a single tool. The installed plugin adds no public reference link by default.

Read the source and installation documentation. MIT license; declared requirements: WordPress 6.5 and PHP 7.4 or newer. Tested on WordPress 7.1.3 and PHP 8.5.11.

Recording method: Sewlore measurement-method guide.

Calculations run in your browser. No readings are submitted or saved by these tools. Worked examples are hypothetical; use your pattern instructions and test your own sample.
